Privacy Policy

How WestCPvP handles basic website, Minecraft server, and Discord support information.

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how WestCPvP handles information when you visit our website or play on our Minecraft server. We keep the site simple and only collect what is needed to run, protect, and improve the server.

Last updated: April 28, 2026

Information We May Collect

  • Basic website data, such as browser type, device type, pages visited, and general traffic logs from our hosting provider.
  • Minecraft-related information, such as your username, UUID, IP address, gameplay activity, punishments, reports, and server logs.
  • Discord-related information you choose to share when joining or contacting the community, such as your Discord username and messages sent in our server.

How We Use Information

  • To keep the server online, secure, and stable.
  • To prevent cheating, abuse, spam, botting, ban evasion, and rule-breaking.
  • To respond to support requests, reports, appeals, and moderation issues.
  • To understand traffic and improve the website and server experience.

Third-Party Services

Our website may use third-party services such as Discord links, Discord invite information, Minecraft server status APIs, hosting providers, and security tools. These services may process limited technical information according to their own policies.

Cookies & Local Storage

WestCPvP does not require an account on this website and does not use a store checkout. Some third-party tools, browsers, or hosting services may still use basic cookies, caching, or local storage for security, performance, or functionality.

Data Safety

We use reasonable steps to protect server and website data, but no online service can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Only staff members who need access for moderation, security, or support should access private logs.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact WestCPvP staff through the official Discord server.
